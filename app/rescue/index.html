Furtive Rescue

Your money, with only your 12 words.

Take everything out of your Furtive account and send it to any wallet. This page talks to the blockchain directly and runs entirely on this device. It does not need Furtive to be online.

1

Find your money

Your words never leave this device.

2

Where should it go?

Use a wallet you control, such as Rabby or MetaMask. Double check it: sends cannot be undone.

3

Network fee

Each withdrawal pays a small network fee. It is paid from your Furtive drop-off address, which belongs to you and comes from the same 12 words.

Needed, about
At your drop-off address

Send a little ETH on Robinhood Chain to your drop-off address, from any wallet or exchange. This page notices it by itself.

Sending from your own drop-off address means this withdrawal can be linked to it on the chain. Your money is never at risk.
